About SRL Designden Engineers

Founded in 1998, SRL Designden Engineers emerged from a vision to revolutionize industrial engineering consultancy. Our journey began with a small team of dedicated engineers who believed in delivering excellence through innovation and precision. Over the past 25 years, we've grown into a leading force in the process industry, serving clients across multiple sectors.

Our mission is to provide sustainable, efficient, and innovative engineering solutions that drive industrial progress. We're guided by our core values of integrity, excellence, innovation, and client satisfaction. Our team of expert engineers and consultants works tirelessly to ensure every project meets the highest standards of quality and efficiency.

Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do you primarily serve?

We specialize in process industries including chemical, petrochemical, pharmaceutical, food processing, and manufacturing sectors. Our expertise spans across various industrial applications requiring precise engineering solutions.

How long does a typical project take to complete?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. Basic engineering projects typically take 2-3 months, while comprehensive projects including detailed engineering and supervision can extend to 6-12 months.

Do you provide post-implementation support?

Yes, we offer comprehensive post-implementation support including maintenance planning, operational optimization, and technical troubleshooting. Our team remains available for ongoing consultation and support.

What certifications and standards do you comply with?

We adhere to international standards including ISO 9001, ASME, and API specifications. Our team stays updated with the latest industry regulations and safety standards.

How do you ensure project quality and safety?

We implement rigorous quality control processes and safety protocols throughout project execution. Regular audits, documentation, and compliance checks are integral to our methodology.
